Arm a side + trigger price. When price crosses it the bot pyramids into the move, takes +target $/oz per position, and closes everything if price retraces from the extreme.

Measured on 37,500 real ticks (see analysis/TREND_LADDER_STRATEGY.md): the spread is a fixed 0.24/oz and never tightens. With no directional edge the expectancy is −1 spread per trade, and no target/stop arrangement escapes that. The ladder multiplies cost, not edge — 1 position loses $30/ladder, 10 lose $105. The only thing that can beat the spread is your trigger. So: leave PAPER MODE on, arm it with real calls for a week, and see whether your triggers actually clear 0.24/oz before any money moves.
